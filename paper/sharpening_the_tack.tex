\begin{abstract}
Can we mathematically detect and computationally reverse biological aging using continuous-time State Space Models?
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Aging is a measurable geometric flattening of the macroscopic Waddington basin. Injecting exogenous precision will artificially steepen this basin and rescue failing biological limit cycles.

\section{Methods}
The experimental validation involves evaluating an Energy-Based Model under simulated age-related decline, followed by precision injection.

\section{Results}
