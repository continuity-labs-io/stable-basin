The first stimulus of a session is preceded by genuine quiet. Stimuli 2 through 10 are preceded by 9 to 10 seconds of post-stimulus recovery. If recovery completes within 10 seconds, those two windows have the same rate.

rate in the 1 second before stimuli 2 through 10, pooled
the ratio between them

Rates are events per electrode per second: divide the event count by n_stimuli * n_electrodes * window_seconds. Pool across all 32 electrodes.
